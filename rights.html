United States

CCPA, CPRA, and a patchwork of state laws

What you can demand

In California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and a growing list of other states you can require a company to tell you what it holds about you, delete it, and stop selling it. Outside those states there is no general right, which is why the state you live in matters more here than anywhere else in the world.

The deadline

45 days, extendable once to 90.

The lever that works

Most large brokers comply nationwide rather than check your address, because checking costs more than complying. Ask as though you are covered.

If they refuse

Your state Attorney General. California also allows a private right of action for breaches specifically.

United Kingdom

UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018

What you can demand

A Subject Access Request gets you everything a company holds about you. Erasure under Article 17 makes them delete it. Objection under Article 21 stops them processing it for marketing, with no balancing test — for direct marketing the objection is absolute.

The deadline

One month, extendable to three for complex requests. It is free.

The lever that works

The ICO takes complaints seriously and companies know it. Mentioning that you will escalate is often enough.

If they refuse

The Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. Complaining costs nothing.

European Union

GDPR, in all 27 member states

What you can demand

The strongest data rights in the world. Access under Article 15, erasure under Article 17, objection under Article 21, and portability under Article 20. These apply to any company processing your data, wherever that company is based.

The deadline

One month, extendable to three. Free unless the request is manifestly excessive.

The lever that works

Fines reach 4% of global annual turnover. No other regime has teeth like it, and companies behave accordingly.

If they refuse

Your national Data Protection Authority. You can complain in your own country and your own language.

Canada

PIPEDA, and Law 25 in Quebec

What you can demand

Access to what a company holds, and correction of anything wrong. PIPEDA's deletion right is weaker than Europe's — it is closer to a right to withdraw consent, after which they must stop using it.

The deadline

30 days under PIPEDA.

If you are in Quebec

Law 25 is significantly stronger and includes a genuine right to erasure and to data portability. Quebec residents should cite Law 25, not PIPEDA.

If they refuse

The Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or the Commission d'accès à l'information in Quebec.

Australia

The Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles

What you can demand

Access under APP 12 and correction under APP 13. Australia has no general right to erasure, which is the honest gap — but APP 11.2 requires an organisation to destroy or de-identify information it no longer needs, and that is the clause to cite.

The deadline

30 days, and they may charge a reasonable access fee — though not for making the request itself.

The lever that works

The 2024 reforms strengthened enforcement considerably. Serious invasions of privacy are now actionable.

If they refuse

The Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
