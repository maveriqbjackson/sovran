Only sign if it is true This says we have received no secret order, no gag order, and no demand for user data. If any of that has changed — or if you have been told not to say so — do not sign it. Let it lapse. The silence is the message, and it is the only part of this that cannot be compelled.

Signing out of habit, without reading it, makes the whole thing worthless.

What you are being asked to do

Some legal orders arrive with a gag clause — the recipient is forbidden from saying they got one. A canary works around that. Nobody can be forced to keep repeating something untrue, so if the statement stops being renewed, readers know to treat it as a signal.

You are one of several people who can renew it. That exists so no single person can be pressured into keeping it alive, and so illness, travel, or an ordinary bad week does not trigger a false alarm.

You are not named anywhere public. The site shows that the statement was signed and when — never by whom. Please keep it that way: do not post about holding a key, and do not store it alongside anything that identifies who gave it to you.

The rhythm Every 14 days, with a 7 day grace period. A calendar reminder every 12 days gives you slack. If you cannot sign, someone else with a key can — but check with them rather than assuming.
