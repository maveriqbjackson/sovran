Where to get your export

Instagram & Facebook
Settings → Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions → Download your information. Choose JSON format, all date ranges, complete detail. Arrives by email in a few hours.
TikTok
Profile → Settings and privacy → Account → Download your data. Choose JSON, not TXT.
X / Twitter
Settings → Your account → Download an archive of your data.
Google
takeout.google.com — pick Location History and YouTube at minimum. Warning: this one is enormous.
LinkedIn, Snapchat, Reddit, Discord
All have an equivalent in account settings, usually under Privacy or Data. Every one of them is required to give it to you.
