What we actually store

Everything on our side, in fullYour email address. A random salt. A hash of a key that only proves identity. And a block of ciphertext.

That is the complete list. If this database were stolen tomorrow, the attacker would get a mailing list and a pile of noise.

Your passphrase is stretched through 600,000 rounds of PBKDF2 in your browser to produce two independent keys. The auth key is sent here and hashed again before storage. The data key never leaves your device and is what encrypts your vault with AES-GCM before upload.

We are not choosing not to look. We built it so that looking is not possible. That is Articles I through VI of the Charter, in working code.
