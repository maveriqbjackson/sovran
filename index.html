Find yourself on the sites that sell you

People-search sites publish your photo, your address, your phone number and your relatives to anyone who types your name. You give us a name to search. Candidate profiles come back to your device, and your own phone works out which ones are actually you by matching faces locally.

Your face never uploads. Neither does the answer. And every confirmed match arrives with the removal request already written.

Why it works this wayThe obvious version of this is to crawl the web and index every face in it. That is precisely what Clearview AI built, and it is the most-condemned product this industry has produced — a biometric database of millions of people who never agreed to be in it. So we build the opposite. The only face geometry that exists anywhere in this process is yours, on your own hardware, because you asked for it. There is no endpoint that takes a face and returns a name — not disabled, not built. You cannot look up a stranger, and neither can anyone who takes our servers.

The last word

Name someone you trust. If you die or go dark, they can erase everything SOVRAN holds and fire every removal request you had queued — without ever holding your keys while you are alive. Your record should not outlive you unless you decided it should.

  • Your key is split, so your successor holds a piece that is useless on its own
  • Nothing triggers until repeated proof-of-life checks have gone unanswered for weeks
  • Every stage notifies you first, with a one-tap cancel
  • A mandatory cooling-off period even after your successor confirms

That last part matters more than the feature does. A switch someone else can pull is a weapon pointed at exactly the people this is meant to protect.

Every service that protects your identity collects your identity first. That makes it the most valuable target in the room — for a breach, for a broker, for a subpoena. It is the reason so many privacy tools eventually become the thing they were built against.

So SOVRAN is built to be worthless to anyone who takes it. These are not promises in a policy that changes when the company does. They are the shape of the system. A promise can be broken overnight. An architecture has to be rebuilt.

I

Your photographs stay on your device

Faces are matched locally. The image is never uploaded, and there is no copy of it to lose.

II

Your identity is confirmed, not collected

A third party checks your ID and tells us yes. We never receive the document, the number, or the photograph.

III

Your notes are sealed before they leave you

Encrypted with a key derived from your passphrase. We hold ciphertext and cannot open it — not for a court, not for ourselves.

IV

Nothing you search for is written down

No query logs. No history. No behavioral profile. There is no record of your use to hand to anyone.

V

We never sell, and never take money from anyone who does

No advertising. No data brokers. No investor who needs us to change our mind about that later.

VI

You can end it completely

Delete your account and your email leaves with it. What we hold on you goes to zero, because there was almost nothing to begin with.

VII

We never block you for how you connect

Use a VPN. Use Tor. We will not throttle you, challenge you, or treat you as suspicious for it. Protecting yourself is not suspicious behaviour — it is the behaviour we recommend.

VIII

Nobody is turned away for lack of money

If you can’t afford it, you say so and we believe you. No proof, no application, no review. We will not ask you to document being poor in order to be protected. How this works →

For families

Your child's record started before they could type.

By the time a kid is old enough to consent, thousands of images and details about them are already public — nearly all of it posted by adults who love them. SOVRAN helps parents close that down without ever building a tool that tracks a child.

Where we draw the lineEverything here is about what you have published, never about monitoring your child. We will not build tracking, location sharing, or message reading for kids. Tools like that are one small step from being used against the person they claim to protect.

Audit what you posted

Opt out of the directory

Schools can release your child's name, photo, and address unless you say no in writing. We tell you which form, which office, which deadline, in your district.

Know the emergency path

If images of your child ever surface, the removal routes exist and they move fast. We keep them one tap away so nobody has to learn them in a panic.

What we charge for, and what we never will

A right is free. The work of defending it is not.

You have a right to counsel. Lawyers still bill. The right is what nobody can take from you — the labor is what someone has to do. We charge for the labor and we never, ever charge for the right.

Knowing is always free

What's exposed about you. What rights you hold. What surveillance sits on your street. How to file every request yourself, by hand, without us. Paywalling that would mean selling you the right itself, and we won't.

Doing it for you is the paid part

Filing hundreds of deletions, chasing them to a real answer, and refiling when they quietly repopulate — that is ongoing human and machine work with a real cost. That's SOVRAN+.

And nobody is turned away for lack of money

The people who most need protection are very often the people least able to pay for it — someone leaving an abusive home, someone on disability, someone whose immigration status makes every record dangerous. So if you can't afford SOVRAN+, you tell us, and we believe you. No proof of income, no benefits letter, no case number, no review. A link that says you need it, and the price changes.

We won't ask you to document being poor in order to be protected. Requiring that proof would mean building a database of exactly who is vulnerable — which is the same database this whole project exists to argue against. 10% of every paid subscription funds this tier — not “a portion”, ten percent, and we will raise it before we ever lower it.

Where this goes next

Eight tools are free. The ninth costs money to run.

Everything on this site works today and always will, free. What does not exist yet is the part that does the work for you.

What the money builds

Filing removal requests with the several hundred companies that buy and sell your address, your phone number, your relatives and your history. Then chasing them. Then proving it happened.

Every request produces a receipt — what was asked, of whom, on what date, and what came back. Signed, timestamped, and yours to keep even if we disappear.

What stays free

Every tool on this site. The Photo Check, the Audit, the Map, the Guide, the Passphrase, your Panel, your Account. None of them cost us anything to run, so none of them will ever cost you anything.

Ten percent of every subscription funds paid access for people who cannot afford it. No application and no means test.

The long view

We are the first generation who have to learn this. We will not be the last who need it.

Every year we treat self-preservation as optional, the gap widens between what is known about us and what we know about ourselves. That gap is where the harm lives. SOVRAN exists to close it — and to prove that a tool built to protect people never has to collect them first.
