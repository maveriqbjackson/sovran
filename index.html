The charter

Sovereignty means
no one else holds
the keys.

Every service that protects your identity collects your identity first. That makes it the most valuable target in the room — for a breach, for a broker, for a subpoena. It is the reason so many privacy tools eventually become the thing they were built against.

So SOVRAN is built to be worthless to anyone who takes it. These are not promises in a policy that changes when the company does. They are the shape of the system. A promise can be broken overnight. An architecture has to be rebuilt.

I

Your photographs stay on your device

Faces are matched locally. The image is never uploaded, and there is no copy of it to lose.

II

Your identity is confirmed, not collected

A third party checks your ID and tells us yes. We never receive the document, the number, or the photograph.

III

Your notes are sealed before they leave you

Encrypted with a key derived from your passphrase. We hold ciphertext and cannot open it — not for a court, not for ourselves.

IV

Nothing you search for is written down

No query logs. No history. No behavioral profile. There is no record of your use to hand to anyone.

V

We never sell, and never take money from anyone who does

No advertising. No data brokers. No investor who needs us to change our mind about that later.

VI

You can end it completely

Delete your account and your email leaves with it. What we hold on you goes to zero, because there was almost nothing to begin with.

For families

Your child's record started before they could type.

By the time a kid is old enough to consent, thousands of images and details about them are already public — nearly all of it posted by adults who love them. SOVRAN helps parents close that down without ever building a tool that tracks a child.

What we charge for, and what we never will

A right is free. The work of defending it is not.

You have a right to counsel. Lawyers still bill. The right is what nobody can take from you — the labor is what someone has to do. We charge for the labor and we never, ever charge for the right.

Knowing is always free

What's exposed about you. What rights you hold. What surveillance sits on your street. How to file every request yourself, by hand, without us. Paywalling that would mean selling you the right itself, and we won't.

Doing it for you is the paid part

Filing hundreds of deletions, chasing them to a real answer, and refiling when they quietly repopulate — that is ongoing human and machine work with a real cost. That's SOVRAN+. And a share of every subscription covers people at genuine risk who cannot pay.

The long view

We are the first generation who have to learn this. We will not be the last who need it.

Every year we treat self-preservation as optional, the gap widens between what is known about us and what we know about ourselves. That gap is where the harm lives. SOVRAN exists to close it — and to prove that a tool built to protect people never has to collect them first.
