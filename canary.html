How to read this page

The statement above is re-signed on a fixed schedule. Your browser compares the last signing date against that schedule — we do not compute the status on our end. There is no alarm on a server that could be quietly switched off. If nobody signs, the page goes overdue on your device, not ours.

Green · Signed and currentThe statement was renewed inside the expected window. Nothing has happened that we are legally forbidden from telling you about.
Amber · Overdue, within graceA signing was missed. This is more likely to be illness, travel, or ordinary human failure than anything sinister — but note it, and check back.
Red · LapsedThe grace period has passed with no signature. Treat any assurance on this site as unverified until it is renewed, and act accordingly.

A canary is a weak instrument and we would rather say so than oversell it. Its legal standing has never been fully tested in court. It can lapse through neglect as easily as through a warrant. What it does offer is a signal that is difficult to compel: an order can force silence, but it is much harder to force someone to keep actively repeating something untrue.

More than one person can signThis is not tied to one individual. Several trusted people hold a signing key, so illness, travel, or anything worse cannot cause a false alarm on its own — and no single person can be pressured into keeping it alive.
